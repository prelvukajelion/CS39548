Sun

The Sun is the star at the center of our solar system. Its gravity holds everything together, and its light and heat make life on Earth possible.

Facts about Sun

  • Diameter: about 1.39 million km, 109 times wider than Earth
  • Distance from Earth: about 150 million km, and its light takes about 8 minutes to reach us
  • Surface temperature: about 5,500 °C, while the core reaches about 15 million °C
  • It holds 99.8% of all the mass in the solar system
  • Age: about 4.6 billion years old, roughly halfway through its life
  • It is made mostly of hydrogen (about 73%) and helium (about 25%)

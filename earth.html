Earth

Earth is the third planet from the Sun and the only world we know of that has life. Water covers most of its surface.

Facts about Earth

  • Diameter: about 12,742 km
  • Distance from the Sun: about 150 million km
  • A day lasts 24 hours and a year lasts about 365.25 days
  • About 71% of its surface is covered by water
  • It has one moon
  • Its air is about 78% nitrogen and 21% oxygen
